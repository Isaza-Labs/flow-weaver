# Maintainers

[GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md) describes what maintainers decide. This file records
who they are. `CODEOWNERS` is the machine-readable form of the same information and is
what GitHub enforces; keep the two consistent.

## Steward

release authority. FlowWeaver is published under the NetworkingDev brand; the contact
routes therefore use the `networkingdev.com` domain, as explained in
[GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md).

## Roles

**Release authority.** Approves a published release and its license metadata once the
release checklist is complete. Held by Isaza Labs LLC.

**Security coordination.** Receives private vulnerability reports through the routes in
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) and decides on coordinated disclosure.

**Licensing decisions.** Approves changes to `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, `legal/` and any
per-directory scope notice. The current licensing and ownership decision is dated
14 August 2026 and is recorded in the project dossier.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainership is granted by Isaza Labs LLC. Sustained, high-quality contribution is the
usual path, but contribution does not confer ownership, governance authority or trademark
rights — see [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md) and [TRADEMARK.md](./TRADEMARK.md).
