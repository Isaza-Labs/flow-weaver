# Assets, data and generated content

## First-party assets

FlowWeaver-owned icons, illustrations, screenshots, diagrams, audio, video, fonts and logo files included in the repository are licensed as copyrightable files under Apache 2.0 unless a neighboring notice says otherwise. Trademark use remains governed by `/TRADEMARK.md`.

Each asset should have an entry in `assets/ASSET_MANIFEST.csv` with creator, source, date, license, modification status and trademark sensitivity.

## Third-party assets

Do not copy assets from search engines, vendor websites or social networks without an applicable license or written permission. Record required attribution and distribution conditions.

## Examples and fixtures

Examples and test fixtures must be synthetic, anonymized with an irreversible process, or supplied under documented authorization. Remove credentials, customer identifiers, IP addresses, hostnames, tokens and personal information.

First-party synthetic examples are Apache-2.0. A sample derived from external documentation or customer data keeps the restrictions attached to its source and may be unsuitable for the public repository.

## User and customer content

Workflows, prompts, credentials, uploaded files, logs and outputs supplied by users are not automatically contributed to FlowWeaver or relicensed under Apache 2.0. Their handling belongs in product terms, privacy notices and customer agreements.

## AI-generated material

Before accepting AI-assisted code or media:

- record the tool and review date when required by project policy;
- verify technical correctness and provenance risk;
- search for suspicious reproduction of third-party material;
- ensure a human contributor can make the DCO certification;
- never include confidential prompts, customer content or model outputs with unclear redistribution rights.

## Models and datasets

Model weights and datasets are excluded from the standard repository license unless an explicit component record approves their own license and distribution. Store large approved artifacts in a controlled registry, not by default in Git history.
