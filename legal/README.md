# Legal and licensing

This directory contains the operational interpretation of licensing, contribution, attribution and distribution rules for the public FlowWeaver repository.

These documents do not replace the Apache License 2.0 text and do not contain private assignments, formation records, identity documents, cap tables or internal legal evidence. Those materials belong in the private corporate dossier.

## Authority order

1. Applicable law and executed agreements.
2. The unmodified Apache License 2.0 text in `/LICENSE`.
3. `/NOTICE` and third-party license obligations.
4. The dated project decision in the private dossier.
5. The operational policies in this directory.

If a policy conflicts with the license text, the license text controls.
