# Contribution terms

## Agreement by submission

When a person intentionally submits a contribution for inclusion in FlowWeaver, and it is accepted, the contribution is provided under Apache License 2.0 unless it is conspicuously identified otherwise and expressly approved before merge.

The contributor confirms that:

1. they created the contribution or have sufficient rights to submit it;
2. identified third-party material is compatible with the proposed use;
3. the contribution does not knowingly include secrets, private data or material subject to undisclosed restrictions;
4. their sign-off may be retained in the public project history;
5. acceptance is at the discretion of Isaza Labs LLC and the maintainers.

## DCO

Every commit must include `Signed-off-by` and thereby certify the Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1 reproduced in [`DCO-1.1.txt`](./DCO-1.1.txt).

```bash
git commit -s -m "Describe the contribution"
```

An employee or contractor must ensure their employer or client permits the contribution. Maintainers may request additional evidence for substantial, security-sensitive or externally funded work.

## No active CLA

FlowWeaver does not currently require a separate CLA. A future CLA must be reviewed, approved by a dated governance decision and accepted prospectively by each affected contributor.

## Contribution marked otherwise

Material explicitly marked `Not a Contribution`, supplied only for discussion, or attached solely as a defect reproduction is not accepted for inclusion until its licensing is clarified.
